# 🌱 AgroGuard AI

### AI-Powered Crop Disease and Pest Detection System

AgroGuard AI is a smart agriculture solution designed to help farmers
detect crop diseases and pest infestations at an early stage using
Artificial Intelligence and Image Processing.

The system analyzes images of crops/leaves and identifies possible
diseases or pest-related problems. It also provides suitable
management recommendations to help farmers protect their crops.

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Crop diseases and pest infestations can significantly reduce crop
yield and cause financial losses to farmers.

Currently, disease identification often depends on manual inspection
or expert consultation, which can be time-consuming and difficult for
farmers in rural areas.

There is a need for a fast, accessible and intelligent system that
can detect crop diseases at an early stage and provide useful
recommendations.

---

## 💡 Our Solution

AgroGuard AI uses AI-based image analysis to:

- 📷 Analyze crop/leaf images
- 🔍 Detect diseases and pest infestations
- 🧠 Classify the detected problem
- 📊 Show confidence/prediction results
- 💊 Provide disease management suggestions
- 🌾 Help farmers take early preventive action

## 🔄 System Workflow

1. Farmer uploads a crop/leaf image.
2. Image preprocessing is performed.
3. AI model analyzes the image.
4. Disease or pest is identified.
5. Prediction confidence is displayed.
6. Recommended management/prevention steps are provided.
